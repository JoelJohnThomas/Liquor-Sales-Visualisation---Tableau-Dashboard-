# 🥃 Liquor Sales Dashboard — Tableau

> An interactive Tableau dashboard that looks at **100,000 liquor sales transactions** across **68 categories** from **January 2014 to February 2015**: what sells, who sells it, and when sales changed.

## 📦 Dataset at a Glance

| 📊 Attribute | 📝 Details |
|---|---|
| 🧪 **Source** | Synthetic liquor sales data |
| 📅 **Period** | Jan 2014 → Feb 2015 |
| 🧾 **Rows** | ~100,000 transactions |
| 🍾 **Categories** | 68 liquor categories |
| 💰 **Key measures** | Bottles sold, sales revenue |
| 🏷️ **Key dimensions** | Date, category, vendor |

> ⚠️ **Note:** The data is synthetic. It shows the analysis approach, not real market figures.

---

## 💡 Key Insights

### 🏆 1. Three categories make up half the market
The **top 3 categories** account for **almost 50%** of all bottles sold:

| Rank | Category |
|:---:|---|
| 🥇 | 80 Proof Vodka |
| 🥈 | Canadian Whiskies |
| 🥉 | Spiced Rum |

➡️ Bottle volume is **heavily concentrated** in a few categories, a typical Pareto (80/20) pattern.

### 💵 2. Six types bring in half the revenue
By revenue, the **top 6 liquor types generate 50% of total sales**, out of 68 categories.

➡️ About **9% of categories** produce **half the revenue**.

### 📉 3. Sales dip from mid-May 2014
The time series shows sales **declining from mid-May 2014** onwards.

➡️ This would be worth investigating further, for example seasonality, pricing changes or supply issues.

### 🤝 4. Top vendor–category combinations

| 🍸 Category | 🏢 Leading Vendor(s) |
|---|---|
| Spiced Rum | Diageo America |
| Canadian Whiskies | Constellation Wine Co. & Diageo America |
| Whiskey Liqueur | Sazerac Co. Inc. |
| Tequila | Próximo |
| Imported Vodka | Bacardi & Pernod Ricard USA |

➡️ **Diageo America** leads in **two of the biggest categories** (Spiced Rum and Canadian Whiskies), which makes it a key vendor in this market.

---

## 🧭 Dashboard Features

- 📊 **Pareto chart**: bars of sales by category with a **cumulative % line** that shows how quickly sales add up across categories
- 📈 **Sales time series**: sales over time, which shows the mid-2014 dip
- 🏢 **Vendor × category view**: which vendors lead each category
- 🎛️ **Interactive filters**: drill down by category, vendor and date and county
